Measurement Systems & Conversion Formulas

Metric vs Imperial Units

The metric system is decimal-based (meters, grams, liters), while the imperial system uses traditional British and US customary units (miles, pounds, feet).

Temperature Calculations

Converting between Celsius and Fahrenheit uses the exact formula: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Absolute zero is benchmarked at 0 Kelvin (-273.15°C).

Binary Digital Storage (Base-2)

Unlike decimal metric prefixes where 1 kilo = 1,000, computer data storage operates on powers of two: 1 Kilobyte = 1,024 Bytes, and 1 Megabyte = 1,024 KB.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many feet are in a meter?

1 meter equals approximately 3.28084 feet (or 39.37 inches).

How do I convert pounds (lbs) to kilograms (kg)?

Divide the weight in pounds by 2.20462. For example, 150 lbs is approximately 68.04 kg.

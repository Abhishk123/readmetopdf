Frequently Asked Questions

How does leap year affect age calculation?

A leap year has 366 days instead of 365. The calculator dynamically considers whether February 29th falls within your age interval.

Can I calculate age for historical dates?

Yes. You can select any Gregorian calendar date to find the time difference between historical events.

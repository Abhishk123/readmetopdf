Financial Planning Formulas & Guides

How EMI Is Calculated

The standard Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) formula applies reducing balance interest, where early payments cover more interest and later payments pay down principal.

The Power of Compounding

In Fixed Deposits and PPF, interest is earned on both the initial principal and accumulated interest, generating exponential wealth over multi-year horizons.

Tax-Saving with PPF

Public Provident Fund (PPF) deposits qualify for tax deductions under Section 80C, with both interest and maturity payouts 100% tax-free under Indian law.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay extra principal to reduce my loan EMI?

Yes! Making prepayments directly reduces your principal loan balance, which dramatically cuts down total interest paid and shortens loan duration.

How often is FD interest compounded?

Most commercial banks compound Fixed Deposit interest quarterly (every 3 months), giving a slightly higher effective annual return than simple interest.

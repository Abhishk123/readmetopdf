How Is College GPA Calculated?

Weighted Credit Hours

A 4-credit calculus course impacts your GPA twice as much as a 2-credit seminar. Multiplying credit hours by grade points produces weighted grade point totals.

The 4.0 Standard Scale

In the standard US grading system, an A is 4.0, B is 3.0, C is 2.0, and D is 1.0. Pluses and minuses typically add or subtract 0.3 grade points.

10.0 CGPA to Percentage

In many Indian and global universities (such as CBSE/AICTE), standard percentage conversion uses the formula: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I raise my cumulative GPA?

To improve your GPA, focus on scoring high marks in higher-credit subjects (3 and 4 credit courses), as they carry more weight in the final calculation.

Are my grades stored securely?

Yes. Your course grades are stored locally in your browser cache and never sent to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reduce my photo size to under 100 KB?

Upload your photo, set the quality slider to 60% or select "Max 1280px" dimension. Check the compressed size readout; once it drops below 100 KB, click Download.

Which format is best: JPEG, PNG, or WebP?

WebP provides the highest compression efficiency. JPEG is universally compatible with every portal. PNG is best if your image has sharp text or transparent backgrounds.

Is this image compressor free?

Yes, completely free with no daily limits or watermarks.

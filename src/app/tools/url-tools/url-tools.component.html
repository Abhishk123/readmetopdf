UTM Marketing & URL Best Practices

What Are UTM Parameters?

Urchin Tracking Module (UTM) codes are five variants of URL parameters used by digital marketers to track the effectiveness of online marketing campaigns across traffic sources.

Naming Consistency

Always use lowercase names and underscores (e.g. spring_promo) for campaigns. Google Analytics treats uppercase and lowercase as different campaigns.

Link Hygiene & Cleanliness

Removing click IDs like fbclid and gclid before sharing links on social forums or Slack prevents surveillance leaks and keeps links readable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does adding UTM parameters affect SEO rankings?

No. Search engine crawlers ignore UTM parameters when canonical tags are properly established on destination pages.

Are links shortened through this tool private?

Yes. Shortened links redirect visitors transparently without capturing or retaining private user data.

Percentage Formulas & Quick Rules

Finding X% of Y

The standard formula is: Result = (X / 100) × Y. For mental math, 10% is found by moving the decimal one place left.

Calculating Percentage Change

Formula: ((New Value - Old Value) / |Old Value|) × 100. A positive number indicates growth; negative indicates a decline.

Shopping Discounts

To find the sale price after a 20% discount, simply multiply the original price by 0.80, then add local sales taxes.

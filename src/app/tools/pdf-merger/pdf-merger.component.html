Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any file size limit?

Because the merger runs on your computer's RAM, you can easily combine documents totaling over 100 MB without bandwidth timeouts.

Are password-protected PDFs supported?

Please unlock password-protected documents before merging, as encrypted files cannot be read without authentication.

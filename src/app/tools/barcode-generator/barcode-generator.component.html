Barcode & QR Code Standards & Best Practices

When to Use 2D QR Codes

QR (Quick Response) codes are ideal for website links, restaurant menus, Wi-Fi passwords, and digital payments because any smartphone camera can instantly scan them from any angle.

1D Barcode Formats Explained

Code 128 handles letters and numbers for shipping and logistics. EAN-13 and UPC-A are standard international retail barcodes printed on consumer product packaging.

Print & Contrast Guidelines

Always maintain high contrast between the dark bars and light background. Vector SVGs ensure infinite crispness when printed on product labels, posters, or business cards.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are words counted?

Words are determined by splitting contiguous non-whitespace character clusters. Hyphenated compound words like "well-known" are calculated as standard word tokens.

Is my essay saved or uploaded to the cloud?

No. Your writing stays 100% in your browser session. When you refresh or close the tab, nothing is stored or transmitted.

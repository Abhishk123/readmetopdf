Tipping & UPI Bill Splitting in India

Restaurant Tipping Norms in India

In India, 5% to 10% tip is customary at sit-down cafes and restaurants if a service charge is not already added. Under consumer guidelines, tipping remains completely voluntary.

Delivery & Service Tips

For online food delivery or valet services, flat round tips (₹20, ₹30, ₹50) or 5-10% of order totals are widely appreciated gestures.

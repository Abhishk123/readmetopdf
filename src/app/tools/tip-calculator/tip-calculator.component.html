Tipping Etiquette & Common Practices

Standard Tipping Norms

In the United States and Canada, 15% to 20% is standard for sit-down dining. In many other countries, service charges are already included or tipping is optional.

Even Bill Splitting

Splitting evenly eliminates awkward group calculations when dining with friends, family, or colleagues.

Pre-Tax vs Post-Tax Tipping

Tradition dictates calculating tips on the subtotal before taxes, though calculating on the final total is common practice.
